---
title: Linked Titles
description: Keep the same series from different sources in one library entry and use their chapters together.
---

Linked Titles groups different source versions of the same series. The source titles and chapters
stay separate. Suwatte presents the group as one library title.

## What a Linked Group Does

- The **Primary Title** owns the library entry and supplies its default title and cover.
- **Combined Chapters** uses chapters from all linked titles. You can still view one source alone.
- Read chapters and bookmarks are shared across the group. The group also keeps one resume position.
- You can change the primary title and the order of the linked titles.
- You can unlink a non-primary title without deleting it from its source.

## Link a Title Yourself

1. Open the title details.
2. Open the menu above the chapter list. Select **Manage Linked Titles**.
3. Tap **Add** and search your installed sources.
4. Select the matching title. Confirm **Link**.

In **Linked Titles**, select the primary title. Reorder the **Chapter Priority List** to control
which source wins when two sources have the same chapter.

## Use Combined Chapters

Tap **Chapters** on the title details and select **Combined**.

- **Show All** shows the chapters from every linked title.
- **Primary + Latest** shows all primary chapters. It adds linked chapters that extend past, or are
  newer than, the latest primary chapter.
- **Distinct Chapters** keeps one chapter for each chapter number. The Chapter Priority List selects
  the preferred source.

Downloaded chapters and chapters in progress stay visible when **Distinct Chapters** would normally
hide their duplicate.

## Find Matches in Your Library

**Find Duplicates** is a shortcut that creates Linked Title groups from titles already in your
library.

1. Open the library menu and select **Find Duplicates**.
2. Review each suggested group.
3. Select at least two title groups. Tap **Merge Selected**.

A matching external identifier gives a **Strong Match**. Suwatte selects these titles for you. A
matching normalized title gives a **Possible Match**. Suwatte never selects a title-only match for
you.

The review keeps existing linked groups together. When it links a new group, it keeps the furthest
reading position and combines the read chapters, bookmarks, and collection memberships.
