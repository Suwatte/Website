---
title: Library Labels
description: Add your own labels to source-library titles and use them as filters.
---

Labels let you group source-library titles without adding another collection page.
For example, add a "Weekend" label to titles you want to read when you have more time.
A title can have more than one label.

## Create and Apply a Label

1. Open **Manage Entry** for a title in your source library.
2. Select **Labels**.
3. Select **Create Label**, enter a name, and select **Create**.
4. Tap the label to apply it to the title.

A checkmark means the title has the label.
Creating a label makes it available in the list; tap it to apply it.
Tap a checked label again to remove it from the title.

## Apply Labels to Selected Titles

Select titles in a source-library view and choose **Edit Labels** from the selection actions.

- A checkmark means all selected titles have the label.
- A minus sign means only some selected titles have it.
- An unmarked label is absent from all selected titles.

Tap a label with a minus sign to apply it to every selected title.
Tap a checked label to remove it from every selected title.

## Filter by Labels

Open the library's filter control and select **Labels**.
Include a label to show titles that have it.
Exclude a label to hide titles that have it.
When you include several labels, a title can match any of them.

These labels belong to your source library.
Local-provider tags and genres are separate metadata.

## Rename or Delete a Label

Touch and hold a label in the Labels screen and select **Rename** or **Delete**.
Deleting a label removes it from every title that has it.
The titles remain in your library.
