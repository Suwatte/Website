---
title: Local Library Metadata
description: Find comic details and covers, review matches, and correct your local library's records.
---

Metadata is the information about a book, such as its title, publisher, issue number, and creators.
Suwatte can read information stored with your files and find more details through Comic Vine or Metron.

## Connect a Provider

1. Open **Settings → Manage Providers** in the **Local Library Metadata** section.
2. Select Comic Vine or Metron.
3. Enter the credential requested on that screen.
4. Select **Connect**.
5. Choose a **Primary Provider** on the Manage Providers screen.

Provider choices apply to this device.
Credentials are saved securely in the device's Keychain.
Use **Test Stored Credential** to check a saved sign-in.

## Match New Items

Turn on **Automatically Match New Items** to look for matches as new local books are added.
Turn on **Only over Wi-Fi** to limit matching to Wi-Fi connections.

Suwatte sends comic details to the provider to find a match.
It does not upload your book files.
Incognito Mode pauses matching.

An uncertain match can need your review.
Open **Settings → Metadata Activity** and select **Needs Review** to compare suggestions.
Select **Choose Another Match** when none of the suggestions is right.
Use **Skip for Now** to leave the decision for later.

To correct an existing match, open the local title's menu and select **Change Match**.

## Correct Book Details

Open a local title's menu and select **Edit Details**.
Change the title, summary, publisher, date, or other fields, then select **Save**.

Provider refreshes keep your edits.
Clear an edited field to restore the provider's value for that field.
For creators, enter one person per line in the format `Name | Role`.

## Check Matching Activity

Open **Settings → Metadata Activity** in the **Local Library Metadata** section.
This screen shows matching states, recent activity, and provider delays.

- **Re-match Unmatched Items** searches again for books without a match.
- **Retry Waiting Matches** retries work that is waiting.
- **Refresh Provider Metadata** updates provider details and artwork while keeping your edits and manual matches.

If a provider limits requests, wait until the displayed pause ends.
Repeated retries do not remove the provider's limit.

## Clear Recorded Metadata

**Clear All Recorded Metadata** removes local matches, edits, and recorded details.
It keeps your files, reading progress, provider sign-ins, and manually chosen folder roles.
Details can return after a later scan.

Use this action only if you want to discard the recorded metadata and your corrections.
