---
title: Page and Tap Controls
description: Adjust tap zones, read wide pages, and choose device controls in the image reader.
---

Open settings from the image reader, or open **Settings → Image Reader** for your defaults.
These controls apply to page images, rather than EPUB text or PDF documents.
Use **Applies To** when shown to choose global, source, or title settings.

## Use the Action Panel

Open **Reader Actions** in the reader to see its chapter list, bookmarks, and **Reader Settings**.
The chapter-list button also shows chapter progress.
Use the chapter arrows to move between chapters.

**Lock Rotation** and **Unlock Rotation** change the reader's rotation lock.
The **Live Text** action appears when supported.
**Minimize Reader** appears when the current reading session can be minimized.
Tap outside the panel to close it.

## Adjust Tap Zones

Turn on **Tap Sides to Navigate** to turn pages or move through a vertical chapter by tapping screen regions.
The other tap controls appear when this option is on.

Choose **Horizontal Layout** and **Vertical Layout** to set the arrangement of the regions.
Turn on **Display Navigation Guide** to see the active regions on the screen.

Use **Invert Tap Zones** to swap navigation horizontally, vertically, or in both directions.
For example, horizontal inversion swaps the left and right regions.
Check the navigation guide after making a change.

**Animate Tap Navigation** controls page-turn animation for taps.
Swipe navigation remains interactive.

## Split or Rotate Wide Pages

**Split Wide Pages** shows a wide page one half at a time.
Turn on **Invert Split Order** if the halves appear in the wrong order for the title.

**Rotate Wide Pages to Fit** shows a wide page sideways.
Use **Invert Rotation** to change the rotation direction.

Split and rotate share one choice.
Turning one on turns the other off.
They affect single-page paged reading; double-page mode keeps spreads whole.

## Zoom and Pan

**Zoom Start Position** chooses where a zoomed page starts.
When **Image Scale** is **Fit Screen**, **Auto-Zoom Wide Images** is available.
Its **Zoom To** control chooses page width or screen height.

With **Pan Wide Images** on, navigation taps move across a wide zoomed page before turning it.
This lets you read the remaining part of a page without skipping ahead.

## Set Device Controls

The **This Device** section has controls for **Rotation**, **Keep Screen On**, and **Show Reading Mode**.
**Live Text** appears when the device supports it.

These choices apply to all titles on this device.
They are separate from source and title overrides and are not copied by reader presets.
