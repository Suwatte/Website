A comic archive and a source chapter are sets of images. The [reading
modes](/docs/reading-modes/) apply to them.

An EPUB file and a PDF file are documents. Suwatte opens them in a separate publication reader
with controls that suit a document.

## EPUB

An EPUB file has a flexible layout. The reader arranges the text to fit your screen. It uses the
structure of the book: the table of contents, the chapter divisions and the internal links.

The text moves when you change a setting. A page number is therefore a result of your settings. It
is not a property of the book.

## PDF

A PDF file has a fixed layout. The reader draws each page as the author made it. It gives you page
controls and a zoom control, not text controls.

## Where they come from

Both formats can come from a [local file](/docs/local-files/). They can also come from a server.
Kavita and many OPDS catalogues hold EPUB files.

## What stays the same

A novel and a PDF are normal library entries. Collections, flags, progress, history and backups
treat them in the same way as a comic. They also go into your reading statistics.
