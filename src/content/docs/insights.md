The **Activity** tab keeps a record of what you read and when you read it.

## Sessions

Suwatte records a session each time that you read. A session holds the title, the time and the
length. All of the other statistics come from your sessions.

## Streaks

A streak counts the days in sequence on which you read. Insights shows it with your progress
toward your daily goal.

## The daily goal

Set a goal in **Settings → Reading → Startup & Activity → Daily Reading Goal**. The value is in
minutes. Set it to **Off** if you do not want one.

Insights shows your progress toward the goal next to your streak.

## Recaps

A recap gives you a summary of a period.

**Recap Source Tap** in **Settings → Reading → Startup & Activity** sets what happens when you
touch a source in a recap.

## Reminders

**Settings → Notifications** has three controls that help you to go back to a title:

- **Reading Reminders** sends a notification after you stop for some time. Set the delay in hours
  with **Remind After**.
- **Quiet Hours** holds a reminder until the window ends. Set **From** and **Until**.
- **Resume Live Activity** shows a Live Activity when you leave in the middle of a chapter. Touch
  it to go back.

If notifications are off for Suwatte in the iOS settings, the app cannot send a reminder. The app
tells you when this is true.

## Incognito Mode and your statistics

With [Incognito Mode](/docs/privacy-controls/) on, Suwatte does not write your progress. That
reading does not go into your streak, your goal or your history.
