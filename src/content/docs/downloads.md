title: Downloads

A download keeps the pages of a chapter on the device. You can then read without a network
connection.

## Download a chapter

Add a chapter to the queue from the chapter list of a title. You can add a range of chapters at
the same time. The queue processes them in the background.

A title with a download shows on the **Downloaded Titles** shelf.

## The settings

Open **Settings → Sources → Downloads**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Max Concurrent Chapters** | Sets how many chapters download at the same time. |
| **Max Concurrent Transfers** | Sets the total number of network transfers. |
| **Only Download on Wi-Fi** | Holds a download until the device has Wi-Fi. |
| **Background Downloads** | Lets a download continue when the app is not in front. |
| **Delete Source Download Upon Completion** | Removes a chapter after you read it. |

The chapter, Wi-Fi and background settings apply to source downloads and to server downloads. The
automatic delete applies only to a **source** download.

## Manage the space

The same page reports the space that your downloads use. It lists each server separately under
**Server Download Storage**, with a count of books and a size. Swipe a server to remove its
downloads.

A remove operation does not change your progress. Download the chapter again later and continue
from your position.

## Downloads, backups and sync

**A download is not in a Suwatte backup, and it does not sync through the app database.** Download
it again on another device.

After you restore a backup or sign in on a new device, your library and your progress come back.
Download again what you want to read offline.
