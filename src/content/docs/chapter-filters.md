---
title: Chapter Filters
description: Filter a source title's chapters and hide split parts when a whole chapter is available.
---

Open a source title's chapter list, then open its ellipsis menu and select **Filters**.
You can show downloaded, read, unread, new, or bookmarked chapters.
Choose **All** to return to the full list.
Use **Group By Volume** to organize the list by volume.

## Hide Split Chapters

Turn on **Hide Split Chapters** to hide fractional chapters when their whole chapter is also listed.
For example, if chapter 1 is listed, chapters 1.1 and 1.2 are hidden.
If chapter 1 is absent, those parts stay visible.

This choice is saved for the title and affects its reading queue.
It also affects the chapters chosen by automatic downloads.

A fractional number can be an extra chapter rather than a split part.
For example, chapter 10.5 is hidden if chapter 10 is listed.
Turn the option off when you want to read those extras.

The control is available on an individual source's chapter list, rather than a combined chapter list.
