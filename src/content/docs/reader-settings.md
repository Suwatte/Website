title: Reader Settings

---

There are two ways to the reader settings. Open the menu in the reader, or go to
**Settings → Reading → Reader Settings** for the defaults.

Many settings have an **Applies To** control. Use it to set a value for the current title, or for
all titles.

## Fit the page

- **Image Scale** selects `Fit Screen`, `Fit Height`, `Fit Width` or `Smart Fit`. Smart Fit decides
  for each page from its shape. Use it when a chapter has single pages and double pages together.
- **Page Fit** and **Enable PillarBox** control the empty space around a page.
- **Crop Whitespaces** removes the empty margins of a scanned page.
- **Downsample Images** lowers the memory that a large page needs. The page is then less sharp.

## Double pages

- **Double Page** shows two pages together, as a printed book does.
- **First Page Alone** keeps the cover on its own, so that the other pages pair correctly.

## Move through a chapter

- **Tap Sides to Navigate** turns a page when you touch the edge of the screen.
- **Navigation Region Style** selects the arrangement of the tap regions.
- **Invert Navigation Regions** exchanges forward and back.
- **Display Navigation Guide** draws the tap regions on the screen.
- **Navigation Haptics** and **Haptic Feedback** give you a physical response.

## Continuous modes

- **Auto Scroll** moves the page down at a constant rate.
- **Page Gaps** and **Page Spacing** put a space between the pages of a vertical layout.
- **Transition Pages** shows a marker between two chapters.

## Colour

- **Use Custom Background** and **Background Color** set a colour that is not black or white.
- **Grayscale** and **Invert Colors** change the page colours.
- **Enable Custom Overlay**, **Overlay Color** and **Overlay Blend Mode** put a tint on the page.
  Use a tint to lower the glare at night.

## Global chapter settings

**Settings → Reading → Global Chapter Settings** controls the chapter list, not the page view. Set
the default sort order and the default filters for each title there.

## Stop in the middle of a chapter

You can make the reader small and do something different in the app. Open it again at the same
page.

If **Resume Live Activity** is on in **Settings → Notifications**, you can leave the app and go
back with a Live Activity.
