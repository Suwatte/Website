---
title: Reader Presets
description: Save a set of image-reader settings and apply it to a title, a source, or your defaults.
---

A reader preset saves image-reader settings under a name.
For example, save one preset for manga and another for long vertical chapters.
Applying a preset copies its settings to your defaults, a source, or a title.

## Save a Preset

1. Open settings from the image reader, or open **Settings → Image Reader** for your defaults.
2. Choose **Global**, **Source**, or **Title** under **Applies To**, when that control is shown.
3. Adjust the settings you want to save.
4. Open **Presets** and select **Save as Preset**.
5. Enter a name and select **Save**.

The preset saves the settings shown on the screen.
This includes unchanged values from the source settings or global defaults.

## Apply a Preset

1. Open image-reader settings for the destination where you want the preset.
2. Choose where the settings apply under **Applies To**.
3. Open **Presets** and choose a saved preset under **Apply Preset**.

**Title** settings take priority over **Source** settings.
Source settings take priority over **Global** defaults.
Applying a global preset does not replace a title or source override.

Source and title choices appear when you open settings for a source title.
The defaults screen does not show title or source choices.

## After You Apply It

The destination keeps a copy of the settings.
Deleting the saved preset does not undo settings that you already applied.
To keep another version, adjust the settings and save a new preset.

Presets save the image-reader settings that can apply to a title or source.
They do not include the **This Device** controls, the separate default reading-mode choice, or book and PDF settings.

## Delete a Preset

Open **Presets → Delete Preset** and choose the preset to delete.
