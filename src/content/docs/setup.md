title: Install the app
description: Get Suwatte on your device, complete the first-run setup, and find your way around.
---

Suwatte needs **iOS 17** or a later version. It runs on an iPhone and on an iPad.

## The first run

The app shows a guided setup the first time that you open it. The setup covers three decisions:

1. **Content settings.** Keep Safe Mode on or turn it off. Start in Incognito Mode or do not.
2. **Where content comes from.** Add a source list, connect a server, or select a folder of local
   files.
3. **Restore a backup.** If you come from a different device, do this now. Do not configure the app
   two times.

To see the setup again, go to
**Settings → Getting Started → Replay Onboarding**.

## The tabs

Suwatte opens to one of four destinations:

- **Library** holds what you saved, in your collections.
- **Home** shows the home pages of the sources that you installed.
- **Activity** shows your history, your sessions, your streaks and your recaps.
- **Updates** lists the new chapters of the titles that you follow.

To change the first destination, go to
**Settings → Reading → Startup & Activity → Open To**.

## Sign in to iCloud

Suwatte syncs your library through your own iCloud account. Sign in to the same Apple Account on
each device, and turn iCloud Drive on.

Downloads do not sync through the app database. Credentials can sync separately through iCloud
Keychain. See [iCloud sync](/docs/sync/).

## Restore a backup

1. Go to **Settings → Advanced → Backups**.
2. Import the backup file.
3. Select a mode. **Replace** removes what is in the app now. **Merge** adds what is missing.

A backup does not hold your downloaded chapters. Download them again on the new device.

Read [Backups](/docs/backups/) for the full list of what a backup holds.

## Appearance

Open **Settings → Reading → Library & Appearance**:

- **Accent Color** changes the colour of the app.
- **Tile Style** selects `Compact` or `Separated` cover tiles.
- **Items Per Row** sets a value from 2 to 12, for portrait and for landscape.
